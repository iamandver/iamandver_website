Tools & Equipment

Programming

Things I use for Programming. These tools have helped me tremendously over the years to learn programming and to write better code.

JetBrains

Absolutely love their IDEs. I think I'm a little addicted to fine-tuning my color scheme.
Godot

Simply the best game engine out there.
C#

It's probably sad to mention my favorite programming language, but C# was the language that I learned to program with, and it has a special place in my heart.

Music

Things I use for making music.

Logic Pro

I am an Apple guy, so naturally Logic is my DAW of choice. Apple’s subscription announcement makes me a bit nervous though. I like owning my things so maybe I'll switch to Bitwig Studio.
Neural DSP

I use the Quad Cortex Mini for guitar. I really miss tube amps but what can you do? Damn neighbors.
JHS Pedals

Best pedal brand IMHO and Josh Scott's YouTube channel is a lot of fun. Go check it out.

Graphics

I did a lot of graphic design here and there. I sometimes make pixel art too, even though I can't draw for shit.

Aseprite

The best software for creating pixel art. Nothing really comes close.
Affinity

I actually preferred Affinity 2 before Canva took over. Ever since the quality has been declining, but it's still miles better than Adobe.

Credits

I like to give credit where credit is due, especially when it comes to the tools and people who inspire me to do what I do. <3

Oldschool PC Font Pack

The ToshibaSat 9x16 pixel font comes from VileR's font pack. It's the only retro font I found that is actually somewhat readable.
Lospec

Awesome source of color palettes mainly for pixel art, but I sometimes use it for graphic design too.
AndroidArts

I find Arne's art fascinating. He is my absolute favorite pixel artist. His website is such an inspiration.
